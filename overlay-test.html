Overlay test

Does text on top of a background image survive Gmail and Outlook? Test both versions.

How to test

  1. Click Copy A, paste it into your signature editor (Gmail: Settings › See all settings › Signature), save, and send yourself an email.
  2. Do the same with Copy B.
  3. For each one, check: is the left panel, the red/navy bar and the icon row still behind the text? Is anything doubled, cut off or white?
  4. If you can, also try classic Outlook on a PC and Outlook on the web.

A · Sharp backgrounds (double size, scaled down)

Sharpest if it works. Fails if the app keeps the background but drops its size: the background then shows twice as big.

B · Real-size backgrounds

Works even if the app drops the size setting. The background is a little softer; the text is just as sharp.
